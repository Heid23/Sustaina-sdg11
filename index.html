The goal in four words

Inclusive

Everyone counts. People of every age, income, and ability have a place and a voice.

Safe

Free from unsafe buildings, hazards, crime, and dangerous roads.

Resilient

Able to prepare for and bounce back from shocks like typhoons and floods.

Sustainable

Meets today's needs without harming the environment for the future.

World Risk Index score (Philippines)

Source: WorldRiskReport (Bündnis Entwicklung Hilft and IFHV), as reported by news: 2024 ABS-CBN, 2026 Daily Tribune.

How SDG 11 came to be

March 2015
The Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015 to 2030 is adopted, guiding how countries cut disaster losses.
September 2015
UN member states adopt the 2030 Agenda, which includes the 17 Sustainable Development Goals.
October 2016
The New Urban Agenda is adopted at the Habitat III conference in Quito, Ecuador, setting a shared vision for cities.
2030
The target year for achieving the Sustainable Development Goals.
