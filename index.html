What is SDG 11?

The United Nations set 17 Sustainable Development Goals. SDG 11 is about making cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable.

Why it matters: more and more people live in cities, so how a city handles homes, transport, waste, air, green spaces and disasters shapes daily life for everyone.

The 6 big ideas (tap to open)

Safe and affordable homes

Everyone should have a safe home with basic services like water and electricity. Problem: fast growth makes housing costly. Solution: affordable housing, upgrading crowded neighborhoods, and building in safe locations.

Easy, clean transport

People need safe, affordable and accessible ways to get around. Problem: traffic and fumes. Solution: reliable public transport, safe sidewalks, bike lanes and cleaner vehicles.

Inclusive city planning

Plans work best when residents, including children, elders and people with disabilities, help shape them. Problem: some groups are left out. Solution: community consultations and accessible design.

Ready for disasters

Typhoons, floods and earthquakes hit unprepared communities hardest. Solution: early-warning systems, hazard maps, drills, and flood-friendly drainage and green areas.

Cleaner air and less waste

Cities should reduce their environmental harm. Problem: smoke, trash and polluted waterways. Solution: waste segregation, recycling, composting and cleaner energy.

Green and public spaces

Parks, trees and plazas cool the city and give everyone a safe place to rest and play. Solution: protect existing green spaces and create pocket parks and community gardens.
